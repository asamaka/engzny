How this works. Built from public AIS ship-transponder data (via aisstream.io). We show only vessels that actually moved through the strait in the last 48 hours — not the usual map full of anchored ships — with their direction and a fact-based open/closed read from transit counts vs the normal ~100 ships/day.

Inbound = heading west into the Persian Gulf · Outbound = heading east to the Gulf of Oman. Data is collected continuously and may lag a few minutes. Not for navigation.
